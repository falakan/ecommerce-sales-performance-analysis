# E-Commerce Sales Performance Analysis

An end-to-end e-commerce sales analysis and dashboard project built in Microsoft Excel to evaluate revenue performance, customer behavior, product performance, and geographic concentration.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes an e-commerce transaction dataset containing approximately 20,000 orders, 4,300 customers, and 5.5 million units sold.

The analysis was designed to answer key business questions around:

- Overall sales performance
- Monthly revenue trends
- Product-level revenue contribution
- Customer revenue and order frequency
- Geographic revenue concentration
- Average Order Value (AOV)

## Tools & Techniques

- Microsoft Excel
- Data preparation and cleaning
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- XLOOKUP
- Distinct counts
- KPI calculations
- Customer and product ranking
- Data visualization
- Business insight generation

## Key Findings

### 1. Revenue peaked in November 2011
Monthly revenue reached approximately **£1.51M in November 2011**, the highest monthly revenue in the dataset.

### 2. Product revenue is concentrated
The top 5 products generated approximately **47.5% of the revenue generated by the Top 10 products**, indicating significant concentration among leading products.

### 3. The UK dominates revenue
The UK generated approximately **£8.77M**, representing roughly **87.3% of product revenue** in the geographic analysis.

### 4. Customer performance varies significantly
Customer-level analysis revealed substantial differences in both product revenue contribution and order frequency, highlighting opportunities for customer segmentation and targeted retention strategies.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize inventory availability and promotional activity for high-performing products.
- Investigate the characteristics driving the performance of leading products.
- Develop strategies to diversify revenue beyond the UK market.
- Use customer-level revenue and order-frequency analysis to support targeted retention and promotional campaigns.
